Training data notice

The maps, coordinates, landmarks and routes are synthetic teaching data, not a live map or verified real-world route. GPS mode requests browser location only after you choose it; synthetic mode works without location permission or an internet connection. Do not use this training app as the sole aid for real-world navigation.
