3. Land Navigation Simulator

- Display a map with a starting point, destination, and checkpoints.
- Let users select a bearing and travel distance.
- Simulate movement and show the resulting position.
  Level: Intermediate · Interactive simulation

4. Distance, Time & Speed Calculator

- Calculate distance from speed and time.
- Calculate estimated travel time between two positions.
- Convert kilometres, metres, nautical miles, knots, and kilometres per hour.
